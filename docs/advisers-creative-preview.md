# Accountants & Advisers: creative preview

Review URL path: `/design-preview/accountants-advisers/index.html`.

This standalone design study preserves and refines the existing workspace artifact at
`/workspace/design-previews/accountants-advisers-v2`. It does not replace the live
directory, change its 111 records, or introduce new backend, payment or map services.

## Direction

A green divider opens into a softly textured ledger. A single search leads charities
into the directory; a dark green membership invitation gives accountancy firms an
equally immediate starting point. A five-firm editorial showcase demonstrates the
visibility on offer. The directory follows as quiet alphabetical rows with a compact
map, while a shortlist follows visitors after they save their first firm.

On mobile the search comes first, followed by the membership invitation. Burton
Sweet spans the showcase width; the remaining four firms use two columns. The map
can be opened above the results. Product navigation links to the existing platform.

## Working preview interactions

- Search six representative real firms by name, sample location or listed postcode.
- Filter by initial letter, select a sample map pin, and highlight its directory row.
- Save/remove firms, open the shortlist, inspect profile treatments and the £39 offer.
- Open the full existing directory of 111 firms using the link below the sample list.
- Use the existing platform login/dashboard route from the header.

## Deliberately awaiting design approval

The map is labelled as an illustrative location treatment. Postcode geocoding,
full-data search wiring, the chosen map provider, paid membership and firm claims
are not implemented here. The price is a proposed £39/month offer, with no checkout.
The five showcase placements are clearly labelled illustrative, not paid partners
or endorsements. ECOVIS, Moore Kingston Smith, Forvis Mazars and Lanham & Francis
are samples in this study; they are not added to the source directory records.
